# 🌱 Precision Agriculture AI

## 🚀 Overview
Precision Agriculture AI is a climate-aware decision system that helps farmers optimize irrigation, reduce water waste, and adapt to changing environmental conditions.

---

## 🌍 Problem
- Unpredictable climate patterns
- Water scarcity
- Low crop productivity
- Lack of real-time decision support for farmers

---

## 💡 Solution
We built an AI-powered system that:

- Predicts irrigation requirements
- Assesses climate risk
- Provides actionable farming insights

---

## 🧠 Key Features

### 💧 Smart Irrigation
Optimizes daily water usage based on environmental conditions.

### ⚠️ Climate Risk Prediction
Identifies potential crop stress due to heat or low rainfall.

### 🌍 Climate Impact Analysis (SDG 13)
Provides insights on drought, flooding, and climate stability.

### 📢 Advisory System
Gives simple, actionable recommendations to farmers.
